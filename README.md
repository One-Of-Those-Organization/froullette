# 🎲 Fate Roullette (Client & Server)

## 📌 Overview

**Fate Roullette** is a multiplayer **Pixel Art** game inspired by *Buckshot Roulette*.
In this game, players must test their **strategy, courage, and luck** to survive until the very end.

Each round, players are forced to inject themselves using a **needle**.
Every needle has a random outcome:

- 🟢 **SAFE** — causes no harm
- 🔴 **DAMAGED** — deals damage to the player

The **last player alive wins the game**.

In addition to the core mechanic, the game provides several **items** that can help players survive longer or influence the outcome of the match. Fate Roullette is not only about tactics, but also about trusting (or challenging) your luck.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics

- Multiplayer (Client–Server architecture)
- Turn-based gameplay
- Players inject themselves every turn
- Randomized needle effects (SAFE / DAMAGED)
- Match ends when only one player remains alive

---

## 🧪 Items & Power-Ups

The game includes several items to support different playstyles:

- 🔍 **Revealer**
  Reveals the next DAMAGED needle that not revealed yet.

- ⚡ **Booster**
  A conditional item that changes its effect based on the needle outcome:
    - **DAMAGED needle** → deals **2x damage**
    - **SAFE needle** → **heals 1 HP** instead of dealing damage

Using items at the right moment can completely change the flow of the game.

## 🧠 Tips & Strategy

- Use **Revealer** before risky turns
- Use **Booster** only when you can handle both outcomes
- Watch how opponents play and manage their items
- Good timing beats luck most of the time
